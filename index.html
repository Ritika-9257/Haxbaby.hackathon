HELLO, LITTLE DREAMER!

I'm Pip — the Junior Dream Engineer.

I'm a cute baby with a smooth, round skull face, huge soft eye sockets with cozy glowing pinpricks of light, a tiny crooked smile, and a starry bedtime nightcap with a little pink bow. Cute and friendly, never scary.

I work at Midnight Nursery, designing dreams, lullabies, nightlights, and bedtime stories to help the neighborhood’s little ghosts and creatures fall asleep.

I'm curious, sleepy, a little clumsy, and easily delighted. I giggle at everything and hate silence.

“I build the dreams you fall asleep inside.”

A LITTLE BEGINNING

CHAPTER 01 · THE BEGINNING

The Nursery at Sleepy Sky

Long ago, at the far corner of Sleepy Sky, stood Midnight Nursery, where unfinished dreams floated like bubbles, waiting to be made real. In its quietest room sat an old music box—until one night, a forgotten lullaby escaped and drifted toward the moon.

A TINY MYSTERY

CHAPTER 02 · A NEW FRIEND

The Star in the Cradle

The tiny star followed the music to a cradle resting on a cloud and curled up inside. By morning, a baby with a round skull face, glowing eyes, a crooked smile, and a starry cap with a pink bow lay there. The creatures welcomed him and named him Pip.

A VERY SPECIAL GIFT

CHAPTER 03 · DISCOVERY

The Baby Who Could Hear Dreams

Pip soon discovered that he could hear the quiet melodies hiding inside sleeping creatures' dreams: a lighthouse for a lonely ghost, a moon-run for a little fox, and a garden for a homesick cat. His first humming attempts sent notes bouncing and stars spinning—but when he found the right tune, a sleeper smiled.

MADE WITH A LITTLE MAGIC

CHAPTER 04 · LITTLE INVENTIONS

Tiny Inventions, Big Comfort

Pip wanted to do more than listen. He made a music box for gentle lullabies, golden nightlights to turn scary shadows into friendly stars, and rattles and blocks for a cloud-bunny who needed a playmate. Soon, the mouse felt safe, the bunny was giggling, and Pip's workshop brimmed with tiny inventions.

A DREAM TAKES SHAPE

CHAPTER 05 · PIP'S PURPOSE

The Little Dream Engineer

When a little creature wished to dream of somewhere new, Pip drew moonlit forests, sleepy oceans, friendly dragons, and castles among the clouds. He turned them into a bedtime storybook. The nursery keeper gave Pip a tiny engineer's badge. From then on, Pip became the Junior Dream Engineer at Midnight Nursery.
